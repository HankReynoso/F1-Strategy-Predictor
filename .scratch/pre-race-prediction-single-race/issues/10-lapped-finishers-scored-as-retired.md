# 10: Lapped finishers are scored as retired

**What to build:** Score lapped finishers like any other finisher. Today the scorer infers retirement from the last Stint ending before the race distance, so a driver who finished a lap down is marked unscoreable. The loader should carry each driver's finishing status (FastF1's race results give `Status` and `ClassifiedPosition`) on `RaceOutcome`, and the scorer should use it: classified finishers are scoreable whether lapped or not, retirements are not.

**Triage (2026-10-20).**

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Decision:** a driver is scoreable only if they took the chequered flag, lapped or not. Drivers who retired are unscoreable even when officially classified (e.g. Magnussen, Singapore 2024: retired lap 57 of 62, classified 19th). Reason: a driver who retired never completed their Strategy, so scoring the truncated final Stint is guesswork; the chequered flag is a cleaner line than the rule that classifies late retirements.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Score lapped finishers; exclude every driver who didn't take the chequered flag.

**Current behavior:**
The scorer infers retirement from a driver's last Stint ending before the race distance. Any lapped finisher looks retired and is unscoreable, so at Singapore 2024 only 7 of 20 drivers are scored.

**Desired behavior:**
Each starter's finishing status travels with the race outcome. A driver who took the chequered flag is scoreable whether on the lead lap or lapped, provided they made at least one stop. A driver who retired (classified or not), was disqualified, or didn't start is unscoreable. Stint end laps are no longer used to infer retirement.

**Key interfaces:**
- `RaceOutcome`: gains each starter's finishing status (took the chequered flag or not). It belongs to the race outcome, never to `PreRaceInfo`, so it can't leak into predictions.
- The FastF1 weekend loader: fills it from FastF1's race results (`Status`: "Finished" or "+N Lap(s)" means took the chequered flag).
- `compare()`: uses the finishing status to decide scoreability.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A lapped finisher with at least one stop is scored by `compare()`
- [ ] A driver who retired is unscoreable, including one who retired in the last few laps and is classified
- [ ] A finisher who never stopped is still unscoreable
- [ ] The finishing status is part of the race outcome, and changing it never changes a Pre-race Prediction
- [ ] Hand-built tests go through `compare()`
- [ ] A slow test checks Singapore 2024 scores 18 drivers (19 classified finishers minus Magnussen), not 7
- [ ] Ticket 08's Results gain a short note with the corrected Singapore numbers, kept separate from the original "before" record

**Out of scope:**
- Mirrored-tie credit (ticket 14) and Degradation changes (ticket 13)
- Any change to which Strategy counts as the driver's actual Strategy
