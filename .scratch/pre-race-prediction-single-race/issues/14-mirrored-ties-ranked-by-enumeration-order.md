# 14: The scorer counts mirrored ties as top-1 by enumeration order

**What to build:** Make Strategy Match fair when Candidate Strategies tie exactly. Mirrored Strategies (M → H and H → M) share the same probability (ticket 09 accepts the 50/50 split until Circuit Tendency), but `predict()` sorts stably over Strategies generated in S, M, H order. The softer-first mirror is always first, and the scorer counts it as the top-1 pick. The "50/50" is really "softer-first wins", which happens to match what most teams do and so inflates the "before" number.

**Blocked by:** None

- [ ] Tests go through `predict()` and `compare()`

**Triage (2026-10-20).**

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Decision:** split credit for ties. If the actual Strategy is tied with others for top pick, it counts as 1/k of a top-1 match, where k is the size of the tied group. A tied group that straddles the top-3 cut-off gives credit for the share of the group inside the top 3. Reason: fair, independent of enumeration order, and it keeps a single number to compare against Circuit Tendency (ticket 09).

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Make Strategy Match independent of how tied Candidate Strategies are ordered, by giving tied groups split credit.

**Current behavior:**
Equal-probability Candidate Strategies are ranked by the order they were generated in (S, M, H), so the softer-first mirror always ranks top and the scorer counts it as a full top-1 match. At Singapore 2024 both scored top-1 matches were mirrored ties.

**Desired behavior:**
Strategy Match treats Candidate Strategies with equal probability as one tied group. If the actual Strategy is in a tied group, top-1 credit is (top-1 slots the group covers) / (group size), and likewise for top-3. With no ties, credit is 0 or 1 as now. Per-driver scores and the race summary carry fractional credit, and the report shows it (e.g. "top-1: ½"). Changing the order Strategies are generated in never changes any score. The order in which tied Candidate Strategies are displayed should also stop depending on generation order (any stated, deterministic rule).

**Key interfaces:**
- `compare()` results: per-driver top-1 and top-3 become credits in [0, 1] instead of booleans; the race summary totals become sums of credits
- The report: shows fractional credit readably

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] An actual Strategy tied 2 ways for top pick scores ½ top-1 and 1 top-3
- [ ] An actual Strategy in a 4-way tie covering ranks 2–5 scores 0 top-1 and ½ top-3
- [ ] Untied results score exactly as before
- [ ] Shuffling the order of Candidate Strategies in a hand-built prediction never changes any score
- [ ] The summary totals are sums of credits and the report displays them
- [ ] Re-running Singapore 2024 shows the corrected top-1, noted in ticket 08's Results, separate from the original "before" record
- [ ] Tests go through `predict()` and `compare()`

**Out of scope:**
- Breaking mirrored ties in the model (ticket 09, via Circuit Tendency)
- Stop Window scoring
