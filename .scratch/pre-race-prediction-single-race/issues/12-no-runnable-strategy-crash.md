## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Fall back to counting used sets for a driver whose new-only Remaining Sets rule out every Strategy, instead of crashing.

**Current behavior:**
The predictor assumes at least one Candidate Strategy survives Remaining Sets. When none does, the whole prediction fails with an `IndexError` (Baku 2024: Alonso has S=0, M=1, H=0 new sets left).

**Desired behavior:**
For a driver with no runnable Strategy under the new-only count, Remaining Sets are recomputed including used sets seen in that driver's pre-race sessions (counted per Compound, never above the Tyre Allocation), and the driver's Candidate Strategies are filtered against that. The driver's Strategy Prediction is flagged as using the used-set fallback, and the report shows the flag. If even that leaves nothing, the driver gets an unfiltered prediction, also flagged. The rest of the field is unaffected. Because FastF1 has no reliable per-set identity (ADR-0002), the used-set count is an approximation; decide how to count distinct used sets and record the reasoning.

**Key interfaces:**
- `StrategyPrediction` (or equivalent per-driver result): gains a flag saying which Remaining Sets rule applied
- The Tyre Allocation tracker: offers the used-sets-included count
- The report: shows the flag next to the driver

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A hand-built driver with no runnable Strategy under the new-only count gets a prediction from the used-set count, flagged, without affecting teammates or anyone else
- [ ] A driver who still has no runnable Strategy gets an unfiltered, flagged prediction
- [ ] Drivers with runnable Strategies are unchanged (existing tests pass)
- [ ] Baku 2024 can be predicted by the command
- [ ] ADR-0002's amendment (written at triage) still matches what was built; update it if the implementation differed
- [ ] Tests go through `predict()`

**Out of scope:**
- Tracking physical Tyre Sets across sessions (rejected in ADR-0002)
- Changing the new-only rule for drivers it works for
