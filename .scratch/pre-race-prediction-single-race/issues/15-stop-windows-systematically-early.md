Re-triaged against the 2026-10-27 baseline (`results/backtest/2026-10-27_baseline.json`, analysed in `.scratch/full-backtest/issues/24-rerun-full-baseline-via-openf1.md`). Both blockers are done: ticket 13, and the full Backtest spec.

**The premise doesn't hold across the era.** Per-driver predictions for all 80 scored races were rebuilt from the warm cache (identical race summaries to the saved baseline). That gives 738 actual stops on a predicted Strategy, where a window can be checked:

- 252 fell inside the window. 224 came after it (the window was early, this ticket's claim). 262 came before it (the window was late).
- The median actual stop is 3 laps before the window's best lap in green races, and 6 laps before in Neutralised Races. On 2022 FastF1 data the median offset is 0.

So there is no systematic early bias. Singapore 2024 was not representative.

**What the full Backtest shows instead.** The Singapore windows in this ticket (PIA 3–6, NOR 3–6, LEC 1–3) are one shape of a wider problem: windows pinned to the start or end of the race.

- 358 of the 738 windows start in the first 6 laps or end in the last 6.
- Narrow ones there contain the real stop 8% of the time.
- Ones 20 or more laps wide (e.g. "lap 5–57") contain it 94% of the time, and supply 44% of all Stop Window hits.
- The likely common cause is the one this ticket guessed at, Degradation from practice. It is not "Medium too high", though: about a third of all team Degradation rates come out as zero, including more than half of those fitted from Push Laps. A Compound that never wears is run as long as possible, which puts the stop at an edge.
- 71% of actual stops (1,819 of 2,557) are on Strategies that weren't predicted at all, so the Stop Window hit rate mostly measures Strategy Match.
