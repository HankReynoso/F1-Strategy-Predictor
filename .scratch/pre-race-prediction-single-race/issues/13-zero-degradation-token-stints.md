# 13: Zero Degradation produces token Stints, degenerate Stop Windows and many-way ties

**What to build:** Stop zero-Degradation fits from turning into "stop on lap 1" Strategies. **Triage (2026-10-20).**

**Decision:** both fixes. (1) Stop clamping team fits to zero: pull each team's fit toward the field average, weighted by how much running the team has on that Compound. (2) Add a minimum Stint length setting as a safety net. Reason: zero rates come from too little noisy data, so shrinking fixes the cause and stops zeros dragging the field average down; no team runs a 1–2 lap Stint by choice, so a minimum length is a cheap guardrail. Compound pace (the bigger fix) is left for later, since it needs new glossary terms.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Replace the zero clamp on team Degradation with shrinkage toward the field average, and add a minimum Stint length.

**Current behavior:**
Each team's fitted Degradation is clamped at zero. Short noisy runs become exactly 0 (six teams at Singapore 2024), which makes a 1–2 lap Stint on another Compound free. Top picks become "S → M, stop on lap 1–2", tied 4–6 ways, Stop Windows span almost the whole race, and the zeros pull the field average down.

**Desired behavior:**
A team's Degradation estimate on a Compound blends its own fit with the field average, leaning on the field average when the team has little running on that Compound and on its own fit when it has a lot. The field average is computed from the teams' raw (unshrunk, unclamped) fits so noisy zeros don't drag it down. The final estimate never goes negative. No Candidate Strategy contains a Stint shorter than a minimum Stint length setting. Both the shrinkage strength and the minimum Stint length are settings with sensible defaults (record how the defaults were chosen; don't tune against Singapore).

**Key interfaces:**
- `Settings`: two new settings, for shrinkage strength and minimum Stint length
- The Degradation model: per-team estimates blended with the field average instead of clamped
- Strategy generation or simulation: Stop laps that would create a Stint shorter than the minimum are not considered, which also bounds Stop Windows

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A hand-built team with a short run that fits slightly negative gets a positive rate near the field average, not 0
- [ ] A team with plenty of running keeps a rate close to its own fit
- [ ] One team's noisy near-zero fit doesn't lower the field average used by other teams
- [ ] No predicted Strategy contains a Stint shorter than the minimum Stint length, and no Stop Window starts before it
- [ ] On a weekend with very flat Degradation, Stop Windows don't cover nearly the whole race
- [ ] Re-running Singapore 2024 no longer gives any top pick with a stop on lap 1–2; the new numbers are noted in ticket 08's Results, separate from the original "before" record
- [ ] Tests go through `predict()`

**Out of scope:**
- Compound pace (spec Open questions)
- Fuel-scaled Degradation (spec Open questions)
- Tuning settings against results (Backtest work)
- Why Stop Windows are early (ticket 15)
