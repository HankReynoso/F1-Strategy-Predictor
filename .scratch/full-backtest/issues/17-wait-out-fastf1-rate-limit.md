# 17: Wait out FastF1's rate limit instead of excluding races

**What to build:** A full Backtest on a cold cache must finish with every race either scored or excluded for a real reason, never because FastF1's request limit was hit. FastF1 3.8 allows at most 500 uncached API calls per hour across all its data sources and raises `RateLimitExceededError` beyond that. The first baseline attempt (2026-10-22, laptop, cold cache) scored 5 of 92 races: after about nine weekends every remaining race became a `data unavailable` Excluded Race with the detail "any API: 500 calls/h".

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** bug

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Current behavior:**
- The Backtest treats a rate-limit error while loading a race like any other load failure, so the race becomes a `data unavailable` Excluded Race and the run moves on, hitting the limit again for every later race.
- When the loader reads previous seasons' races at a circuit (for Pit Loss and Historical Degradation), it catches every exception from a past race and skips that race. A rate-limited past race is silently treated as "no history". This is the likely cause of 2022 Australia and 2022 Canada being excluded with "no previous seasons' races at 'Melbourne' / 'Montréal'", even though both circuits were raced in 2018–2019; confirm or find the real cause (e.g. another Location rename like ticket 16's).
- FastF1's session loading can also log errors and carry on with partial data instead of raising; a rate-limit hit there could silently produce an incomplete weekend.

**Desired behavior:**
- When FastF1's rate limit is hit anywhere during a Backtest (loading a race, or loading a previous season's race for history), the run pauses until the limit allows more calls, says so on the progress output (with roughly when it will resume), then retries the same race. It never turns a rate-limit hit into an Excluded Race or into missing history.
- The loader distinguishes "this past race genuinely has no usable data" (skip it, as today) from "this past race couldn't be fetched because of the rate limit" (wait and retry, or fail loudly), and never lets a rate-limit hit produce partial weekend data.
- If a race still can't be loaded after waiting (e.g. a real outage), it's excluded with a clear rate-limit or network reason, distinct from genuine "data unavailable".
- Cached loads don't count toward the limit, so a re-run on a warm cache never waits.
- Single-race and Smoke Set modes behave the same way.

**Key interfaces:**
- The Backtest call: waiting and retrying is part of running a race; the loader function passed in may raise a rate-limit error, and the Backtest handles it. A wait strategy should be injectable so tests don't actually sleep.
- The FastF1 weekend loader: past-race loading must let rate-limit errors out (or handle them) instead of swallowing them with other exceptions.
- `ExclusionReason`: gains a reason for "couldn't fetch data (rate limit or network) after retrying", if needed, distinct from `data unavailable`.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] With a fake loader that raises FastF1's rate-limit error once and then succeeds, the race is scored, not excluded, and progress output reports the wait
- [ ] Waiting uses an injectable clock or sleep, so fast tests take no real time
- [ ] A past race that hits the rate limit is never treated as "no history"
- [ ] The cause of 2022 Australia's and 2022 Canada's Pit Loss failures is identified and recorded; if it's a Location rename, it joins ticket 16's alias map
- [ ] A race that still fails after retrying is excluded with a reason that says it was a fetch problem, not missing data
- [ ] A warm-cache re-run never waits
- [ ] The README's full-Backtest section explains the rate limit, gives a realistic cold-cache duration, and says the run can be left unattended
- [ ] Tests go through the Backtest call with a fake loader, plus a slow test for the loader's past-race path where practical

**Out of scope:**
- Raising or bypassing FastF1's rate limit
- Storing the FastF1 cache somewhere persistent
- Any model or scoring change
