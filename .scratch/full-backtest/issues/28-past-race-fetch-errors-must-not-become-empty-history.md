**What to build:** When the router loads a previous season's race for Pit Loss and Historical Degradation, a rate-limit hit or a failed fetch must never quietly turn into "this circuit has no history". ## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Let rate-limit and fetch errors on past races propagate; only genuinely missing data may give empty history, and it must be reported.

**Current behavior (confirmed in the code at triage):**
- The router's per-season past-race fetch wraps both sources in `except Exception` and returns empty `PastRaceSamples`. Its docstring says FastF1's rate-limit error still propagates, but `FastF1CriticalError` is a `RuntimeError`, so the outer `except Exception` catches it. Ticket 17's wait-and-retry never sees a rate-limit hit on a past race loaded through the router.
- On the OpenF1 side, an `OpenF1FetchError` (a fetch that still failed after the client's retries) is swallowed the same way.
- Separately, the OpenF1 past-race loader returns empty samples when it can't match the past race at all (no rows). The router passes it alias strings that are FastF1 `Location` values; if OpenF1 names the circuit differently, the match fails silently.
- Ticket 23's Smoke Set run showed Baku 2024 and Jeddah 2024 with only `(2022,)` history: their 2023 OpenF1 samples came back empty, cause unknown.

**Desired behavior:**
- FastF1's rate-limit error and OpenF1's fetch error on a past race propagate to the Backtest, which waits and retries (ticket 17) or excludes the race with the "fetch failed" reason. Neither ever becomes empty history.
- A past race may still give empty samples only when it genuinely has no usable data (no such race under the alias, or no clean laps), and that reason is recorded where a Backtest reader can see it: through ticket 26's data-quality warnings if it has landed, otherwise in the per-race record and the Backtest summary.
- Matching a past race works for every 2023–2025 circuit across both sources' naming; Baku and Jeddah 2023 are found and contribute samples.

**Key interfaces:**
- The router's per-season past-race fetch: narrow what it catches; let rate-limit and fetch errors through.
- The OpenF1 past-race loader: distinguish "couldn't find this race" from "found it but no clean data", and make the circuit match work with the shared alias map (ticket 16).
- The per-race record in the Backtest Result: carries which past seasons gave no samples and why.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] With a fake past-race loader that raises FastF1's rate-limit error, the Backtest waits and retries instead of producing empty history (fast test, injected wait)
- [ ] With a fake that raises an OpenF1 fetch error, the race is retried or excluded as "fetch failed", never scored on empty history
- [ ] A past race that genuinely has no usable data still gives empty samples, with its reason visible in the Backtest Result
- [ ] The cause of Baku 2024's and Jeddah 2024's empty 2023 history is found and recorded; both races then use 2022 and 2023 history (slow test)
- [ ] For every 2023–2025 circuit, the OpenF1 past-race match is checked against the alias map and any gap is fixed or recorded
- [ ] The Smoke Set is re-run through the router and its numbers recorded in this ticket

**Out of scope:**
- Moving slow tests to the router (ticket 27)
- Data-quality warnings for race-control gaps (ticket 26), except reusing its mechanism if it has already landed
- Any model or scoring change
