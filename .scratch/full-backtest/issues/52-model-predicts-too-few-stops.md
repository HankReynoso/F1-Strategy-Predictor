# 52: The model predicts too few stops

**What to build:** Find out why the top Candidate Strategy is a 1-stop for almost every driver, and fix what's a modelling error rather than a setting. In the 2026-10-27 baseline the top pick is a 1-stop for 79% of scored drivers, but only 40% of them actually ran a 1-stop. In green races alone, where Safety Car Deviations can't explain extra stops, it is 74% predicted vs 42% actual.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately); best judged after 50 and 53

**Status:** needs-triage

**Category:** enhancement

**Evidence (2026-10-27 baseline, rebuilt per driver from the warm cache).**

| Scored drivers | Actual 1 / 2 / 3 / 4+ stops | Top pick 1 / 2 / 3 stops |
|---|---|---|
| Green (481) | 204 / 225 / 49 / 3 | 354 / 122 / 5 |
| Neutralised (897) | 342 / 345 / 143 / 67 | 739 / 139 / 19 |

The circuit breakdown follows stop count almost exactly:

- Strongest circuits, mostly 1-stop races: Jeddah (top-3 65%), Miami (59%), Baku (51%), Marina Bay (47%).
- Weakest circuits, mostly 2–3-stop races where the model still picks a 1-stop:
  - Barcelona: 0% top-1, 0% top-3 over 4 races. 33 of 75 drivers ran 3 or more stops, and 65 of 75 top picks were 1-stops.
  - Lusail: 0% top-1. Every top pick was a 1-stop; every driver stopped 2–4 times. 2023 Qatar also had a mandatory maximum stint length, which the model can't know.
  - Spa-Francorchamps, São Paulo, Sakhir: under 2% top-1.

328 scored drivers ran a Strategy their Remaining Sets allowed but which fell outside the 20 s Plausible margin, mostly multi-stop Strategies the simulation rates as too slow. Pit Loss is not the lever here: at Singapore 2024, moving Pit Loss anywhere from 28 to 34 s changes no score.

Likely contributors to rule in or out:
- Degradation that is zero or negative (ticket 53)
- Linear Degradation with no fall-off late in a Stint
- The 0.03 s/lap fuel effect
- The Plausible margin and probability scale, which are settings

- [ ] The cause is identified across several multi-stop circuits (at least Barcelona, Sakhir, Lusail), not one race
- [ ] Modelling fixes go through `predict()` tests; anything that is only a setting is left to tuning on the Tuning Seasons
- [ ] The predicted vs actual stop-count table above is recorded before and after
