# 53: A third of team Degradation rates come out as zero

**What to build:** Find out why so many fuel-corrected team Degradation rates come out at or below zero (and are then clamped to exactly 0), and fix what's a modelling error. A Compound with zero Degradation never wears. Then the best Strategy runs it for as long as possible, so the simulation prefers fewer stops (ticket 52) and puts Stop Windows at the very start or end of the race (ticket 54).

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

- The shares are similar on FastF1 (2022) and OpenF1 (2023–2025) data. Push Laps clamp most often on both: 65% on FastF1, 51% on OpenF1.
- The median team rate is 0.064 s/lap on the Medium and 0.020 s/lap on the Hard. The Medium wears faster than the Hard for only 55% of team pairs.
- Ticket 13 stopped zero rates from producing 1–2-lap token Stints; it didn't change how often they occur.

Likely contributors to rule in or out:
- The 0.03 s/lap fuel effect over-correcting short runs
- Track evolution through a practice session
- Push Laps used as a Degradation measure at all
- Shrinkage towards a field average that is itself pulled down by clamped zeros
