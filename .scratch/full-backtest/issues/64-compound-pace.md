# 64: Compound Pace in the simulation

**What to build:** Add **Compound Pace** (`CONTEXT.md`) to Simulated Race Time, so a fresher, faster Compound can earn back the Pit Loss of an extra stop. Today every lap costs `base lap time + Degradation × tyre age`, with the same base for every Compound, so a fresh Soft is no faster than a fresh Hard and the model almost always prefers a 1-stop (ticket 52: 74% predicted 1-stops in green races against 42% actual). Measure the possible sources first, then build the best-supported blend.

**Blocked by:** 62 (Compound ordering per team)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Category:** enhancement

## Agent Brief

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Measure where Compound Pace can come from, then add a field-level, per-circuit Compound Pace with a fallback chain to the simulation.

**Decisions already made (maintainer, 2026-10-27):**
- Definition: Compound Pace is how much faster a fresh Tyre Set of a Compound laps than a fresh Hard at the same circuit and fuel load, in s/lap. Hard = 0. Lap time in the simulation becomes base − Compound Pace + Degradation × tyre age.
- Granularity: field-level per circuit first. Per-team (with shrinkage towards the field, like Degradation) is measured as an option, not the default.
- Ordering: Soft ≥ Medium ≥ Hard is enforced, as ticket 62 does for Degradation.
- Source: a blend with a fallback chain, after measuring each candidate on its own:
  - (a) practice Long Runs: fuel-corrected starting pace by Compound
  - (b) qualifying: fresh-tyre gaps, scaled down to race conditions by a factor fitted on the Tuning Seasons
  - (c) Circuit History: starting pace of past race Stints by Compound (same Regulation Era, ADR-0004; thin history as in ticket 47)
- Order: 62 → 64 → 63. Ticket 63 then tunes shrinkage, the floor and any Compound Pace settings in one pass.

**Held-out Season rule:** any comparison used to choose between options is judged on the Tuning Seasons (2022–2024) only. Report 2025 alongside, but never use it to decide.

**Measure first (record in this ticket):**
- For each candidate source, how well it predicts the Compound Pace actually seen in that race (a race-measured reference, as ticket 53 used for wear; used only to evaluate, never as an input), on the Tuning Seasons, across at least eight circuits and both data sources.
- Coverage: how often each source has data for each Compound.
- Decision rule, fixed before building: order the fallback chain by measured accuracy (lowest median absolute error first); drop a source whose error is worse than using no Compound Pace at all. Record the measurements and the chain chosen.

**Then build:**
- Compound Pace per circuit from the chosen chain, with each estimate recording its source (like Degradation sources).
- The ordering rule applied after the chain.
- Any new constants (e.g. the qualifying scale factor) as settings, fitted on the Tuning Seasons only and left for ticket 63 to tune further.
- The single-race report shows each Compound's Compound Pace and source.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Each candidate source's accuracy and coverage against race-measured Compound Pace is recorded per Compound, on the Tuning Seasons, with the decision rule applied as written
- [ ] Simulated Race Time includes Compound Pace; fixture tests through `predict()` show a faster fresh Compound can make a 2-stop beat a 1-stop that it lost to before
- [ ] Soft ≥ Medium ≥ Hard holds for every prediction, with a test
- [ ] Every Compound Pace estimate records its source, shown in the single-race report and counted in the Backtest Result like Degradation sources (follow ADR-0005's versioning rule)
- [ ] An offline full Backtest is compared with the post-62 result: Tuning top-1, top-3, Stop Window hits, and ticket 52's predicted-vs-actual stop-count table, with 2025 reported only
- [ ] The per-team option is measured and reported; adopted only if it beats field-level on Tuning top-3 by more than 0.5 pp
- [ ] ADR-0003 (or a new ADR) records the Compound Pace model and its source chain; the pre-race spec's Compound pace open question points here

**Out of scope:**
- Tuning shrinkage, the Degradation floor and Compound Pace settings together (ticket 63)
- Non-linear Degradation, fuel-scaled Degradation, traffic and undercuts
- Breaking mirrored ties (pre-race ticket 09, Circuit Tendency): a constant Compound Pace adds the same time whatever the Compound order, so it won't break M → H / H → M ties
