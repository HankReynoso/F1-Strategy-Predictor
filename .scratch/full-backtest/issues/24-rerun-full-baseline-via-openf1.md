## Results

### Headline

80 of the 92 race weekends in 2022–2025 were scored: 26 green, 54 Neutralised. 12 are Excluded Races, every one a wet race.

| | Top-1 Strategy Match | Top-3 Strategy Match | Stop Window hits |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Green races (headline, pooled)** | **53.17/481 (11%)** | **126.92/481 (26%)** | **105/813 (13%)** |
| Green races, race-averaged | 11% over 26 races | 26% | 14% |
| Neutralised Races | 98.17/897 (11%) | 223/897 (25%) | 147/1,744 (8%) |
| All races | 151.33/1,378 (11%) | 349.92/1,378 (25%) | 252/2,557 (10%) |

In plain terms, the model's top pick is the Strategy the driver ran about 1 time in 9, and the driver's Strategy is in the top three about 1 time in 4. Pooled and race-averaged figures agree, so no few big races dominate.

Two structural limits sit under these numbers:

- **Top-1 can't be a clean hit.** No scored driver in 80 races has an untied top-1 match. Every Candidate Strategy ties with its Mirrored Strategies, because the simulation doesn't depend on Compound order. All top-1 credit is split credit; the most a correct 2-way tie can score is ½.
- **Most stops can't be Stop Window hits.** 71% of actual stops (1,819 of 2,557) are on Strategies the model didn't predict, and those can never be hits. The Stop Window figure mostly measures Strategy Match.

The split is almost entirely about stop count:

- 28 of the 80 races score 0 top-1; 22 score 0 top-3.

### Green vs Neutralised

Top-1 and top-3 are almost identical in green and Neutralised Races (11% / 26% against 11% / 25%). Stop Window hits drop from 13% to 8%. Neutralised drivers make more stops (23% made three or more, against 11% in green races) and stop earlier than planned (median 6 laps before the window's best lap, against 3 in green races). That costs window hits, but it doesn't change Strategy Match much, because the model's own errors (below) are larger than the Safety Car's effect.

54 of the 80 races are Neutralised, since any VSC counts. 16 of 18 2022 races are Neutralised. So the green headline rests on 26 races.

### What drives the misses

Each driver's prediction was rebuilt from the warm cache for this analysis (see Implementation notes). The 1,378 scored drivers break down as:

| Outcome | Drivers |
|---|---|
| Actual Strategy not a Candidate Strategy at all | 832 (60%) |
| A Candidate, but outside the top 3 | 142 (10%) |
| In the top 3, not top-1 | 69 (5%) |
| In the top tied group (split top-1 credit) | 335 (24%) |
| Clean top-1 | 0 |

In order of size:

1. **Remaining Sets rule out the actual Strategy** (504 of the 832). The actual Strategy needed more sets than the predictor thought the driver had left.
   - On OpenF1 data this is mostly a loader gap. OpenF1 marks re-fitted practice and qualifying sets as new, so 29% of 2023–2025 drivers have more "new" sets than their allocation, and 26% fall back to the used-set or unfiltered count (2% on FastF1). **Ticket 50.**
   - On FastF1 data, where new-set detection is sound, 24% of scored 2022 drivers are still ruled out, which contradicts ADR-0002's "the understatement is small". **Ticket 51.**
2. **Too few stops** (most of the remaining 328, which were runnable but outside the 20 s Plausible margin). The model's top pick is a 1-stop for 79% of drivers, against 40% who ran one (74% against 42% in green races). This drives the circuit split above. **Ticket 52.**
3. **Zero Degradation.** 31% of team-Compound Degradation rates are clamped to 0, including 53% of those fitted from Push Laps. A Compound that never wears favours fewer stops, and pushes Stop Windows to the race edges. **Ticket 53.**
4. **Mirrored ties.** 335 drivers ran a Strategy in their top tied group and got ½ (or less) top-1 each. Breaking those ties correctly would take all-races top-1 from 11% to about 24%. Already owned by pre-race ticket 09 (Circuit Tendency); evidence added there.
5. **Stop Windows at the race edges.** Of the 738 stops on predicted Strategies, 358 have a window touching the first or last 6 laps. Narrow edge windows hit 8% of the time; wide ones ("lap 5–57") hit 94% and supply 44% of all hits. There is no systematic early or late bias (262 stops before the window, 224 after). **Ticket 54**, superseding pre-race ticket 15.
6. **Smaller inputs.**
   - Circuit History was used in 6 races, which score 3.4% top-1 (comment on ticket 47; 2025 China's history is cut short by ticket 33).
   - Pit Loss estimates are off by up to 10 s against later measurements (ticket 55), and wet past races inflate Pit Loss at five circuits (comment on ticket 35).
   - Pit Loss itself barely moves scores: at Singapore 2024 any value from 28 to 34 s gives the same scores.

**Decisions and alternatives rejected.**

- *Rebuild per-driver predictions rather than reason from race summaries.* The summaries can't separate "not a Candidate" from "outside the top 3" or "lost in a tie", and those need different tickets. The rebuild was checked against the saved summaries (all 80 equal) before any number was used. Rejected: running a fresh full Backtest with extra logging, which needs code changes.
- *Cache-only clients for every rebuild.* So this analysis spends no OpenF1 or FastF1 budget and can't silently differ from the cached data the baseline used.
- *Ticket 15 → `wontfix` and a new ticket 54, rather than rewriting 15.* The full Backtest doesn't support 15's premise. The 2026-10-20 triage already expected the work to move to this folder. A new ticket keeps 15's original record intact, as was done with ticket 09 → 24.
- *Tickets 50 and 51 kept separate.* 50 is a data bug with a clear fix; 51 is a modelling decision that reopens ADR-0002 and can only be measured honestly after 50.
- *Tickets 52 (stop count) and 53 (zero Degradation) kept separate.* 53 is the leading suspect for 52 but not proven; 52 also involves settings (Plausible margin) and linear Degradation.

- *Ticket 32 not split.* The not-raced-season wording is the same defect as the cancelled-race wording, so the comment proposes widening 32.
- *"At an edge" means within 6 laps of the start or finish.* That is where `min_stint_length` (5) puts the earliest legal stop. The finding doesn't depend on the exact threshold: narrow windows at an edge hit 8% against 32% for interior ones.
- *No new ticket for sprint weekends.* The sprint gap can't be separated from circuit mix (see Results), so it isn't a clear gap yet. Revisit after 50 and 52.
