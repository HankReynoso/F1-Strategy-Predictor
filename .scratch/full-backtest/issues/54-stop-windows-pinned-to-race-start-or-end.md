# 54: Stop Windows pinned to the start or end of the race

**What to build:** Stop Windows should sit where the simulation's trade-off between Compounds puts them, not at the edge of the race. In the 2026-10-27 baseline, almost half the Stop Windows of correctly predicted Strategies touch the first few or last few laps of the race. Narrow ones there almost never contain the real stop. Wide ones (often "lap 5–57") contain it almost by default, and supply 44% of all Stop Window hits.

Supersedes pre-race ticket 15 ("Stop Windows are systematically early"), whose premise the full Backtest doesn't support; see its re-triage comment.

**Blocked by:** 53 (zero Degradation investigation); the reporting change is ticket 58

**Status:** needs-triage

**Category:** enhancement

**Evidence (2026-10-27 baseline, rebuilt per driver from the warm cache).** Stop Window hits are only possible for stops on a Strategy that was predicted: 738 of the 2,557 actual stops. The other 1,819 are on Strategies that weren't Candidates.

The 738 windows by position ("at an edge" means the window starts in the first 6 laps or ends in the last 6 laps):

| Window | Stops | Hits |
|---|---|---|
| Interior | 380 | 123 (32%) |
| At an edge, under 20 laps wide | 240 | 18 (8%) |
| At an edge, 20 laps or wider | 118 | 111 (94%) |

- No systematic direction: 262 actual stops fell before their window and 224 after it. The median actual stop is 3 laps before the window's best lap in green races, and 6 laps before it in Neutralised Races (Safety Car stops come early).
- 2022 on FastF1: median offset 0.
- 2024 Singapore shows both failure shapes. VER M → H is "lap 5–5" against an actual stop on lap 29. LEC and SAI are "lap 5–57"; both count as hits.

- [ ] The cause of edge windows is identified (zero or near-zero Degradation on one Compound is the main suspect: ticket 53)
- [ ] A window that spans most of the race is either fixed or reported in a way that doesn't count as a meaningful hit; the choice is recorded
- [ ] Consider reporting Stop Window hits over stops on predicted Strategies as well as over all actual stops, so the figure measures windows rather than Strategy Match (a spec change, recorded if made)
- [ ] The table above is recorded before and after
