# 51: The new-only Remaining Sets rule rules out Strategies drivers actually ran

**What to build:** Decide whether Remaining Sets should keep counting new sets only. ADR-0002 accepted the new-only count on the grounds that "the understatement is small in practice because teams protect new sets for the race". The full Backtest says otherwise: even on FastF1 data, where new-set detection is sound (ticket 50), the Strategy a driver actually ran needed more sets of some Compound than their new-only Remaining Sets for 71 of 292 scored 2022 drivers (24%). Those drivers can't score at all, because their actual Strategy is never a Candidate Strategy.

_Contradicts ADR-0002's claim that the understatement is small; worth reopening because a quarter of scored drivers are affected._

**Blocked by:** 50 (on OpenF1 data the over-count inflates this to 40% and must be fixed first)

**Status:** needs-triage

**Category:** enhancement

**Evidence (2026-10-27 baseline, rebuilt per driver from the warm cache).**

- 832 of 1,378 scored drivers (60%) ran a Strategy that wasn't among their Plausible Candidate Strategies. For 504 of them the actual Strategy needed more sets than the Remaining Sets the predictor used:
  - FastF1 (2022): 71 of 292 scored drivers (24%)
  - OpenF1 (2023–2025): 433 of 1,086 (40%), inflated by ticket 50
- The typical cases are two- and three-stop races that reuse Softs from qualifying or a second Hard: Barcelona (actual `S → M → S → S`, `S → M → H`), Sakhir (`S → H → H`), São Paulo (`S → S → S → M → S`).
